# Garden plan

Spring planting for the **raised beds**. Water early in the day and check
`soil_temp` before sowing anything *tender*.

## Beds

| Bed | Plant        |   Sown | Harvest | Notes               |
|:----|:-------------|-------:|--------:|:--------------------|
| A   | Tomatoes     | 12 Mar |     Jul | stake at 30 cm      |
| B   | Runner beans |  2 Apr |     Aug | needs a frame       |
| C   | Carrots      | 20 Mar |     Jun | thin to 5 cm        |
| D   | Courgettes   |  1 May |     Jul | one plant is plenty |

## Jobs

- DONE: order seed potatoes
- TODO: fix the tap on the water butt
- IDEA: herb spiral by the shed
- IMPORTANT: cover the beans if there's frost
